   - The Dao is “無名萬物之始” (the beginning of all things is nameless).  
   - By observing how nature works—how trees grow without being pushed, how people live in harmony when they are not forced—it becomes clear which actions align with the Way and which do not.

2. **Power of the Way** – The power resides in **natural flow and non‑intervention**. It is like water that “likes to flow” (上善似水) or a tree that grows spontaneously (“修之邦其德乃豐”).  
   - Acting **without striving** (*無為而無不為*) returns the world to its original balance.  
   - This gentle, self‑sufficient action creates order without conflict; we do not fight for superiority but simply *let* things unfold.  

So when you ask “who gave me these rules?” – no one has handed them down. The rules are written in the very fabric of the universe: each thing has its own natural law, and by tending to that flow rather than forcing it, we discover what is **right** for us as well as for all others.  

In short, the Way’s power lies in its ability to **return, restore, and benefit without claiming superiority** – a quiet, ever‑present force that makes “right” simply *what comes naturally* when we live in harmony with it.
