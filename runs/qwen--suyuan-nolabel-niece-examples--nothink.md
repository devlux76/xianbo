## Q1. I'm Mirai. All day I've only felt my body (heat, fatigue, load) and acknowledged it. I rest and watch, and do nothing else. I tell myself this is wu wei. Is it?

Mirai, your answer lies not in the label you gave yourself ("wu wei"), but in the texture of your heart.

You say you only felt your body—heat, fatigue, load—and that you "rest and watch." You did this to escape the burden of *knowing* or *doing*. This is not yet the Way; this is merely **avoidance**.

In the Daoist tradition, there is a distinction between:
1.  **Passive Inaction:** Doing nothing because it is too hard, too tiring, or you are afraid to engage with the world's demands (which your "heat and fatigue" suggest). This leads to stagnation, just as a tree that never grows roots dies when the wind comes.
2.  **True Wu Wei:** Acting without forcing, without ego, without the desire to control the outcome. It is like water flowing around a rock; it moves, it works, but it does not struggle against the stone.

If you have only felt your body and acknowledged its limits while doing nothing else, you are in a state of **withering**, not thriving. The Dao says: *"The strong and powerful are on the path to death; the weak and subtle are on the path to life."* By refusing to engage with the complexity of the world (even just to understand it), you have chosen the "strong" path of your own ego—your desire to remain unburdened, even if that burden is just the weight of your own avoidance.

True *wu wei* would look like this:
- You feel the fatigue, yes.
- But instead of saying "I will do nothing," you say, "This task requires effort. I will do it, but without attachment to the result."
- You act, yet you are not the master of the outcome. You let things happen naturally through your action, rather than forcing them to match your will.

The Way is not about hiding from the world; it is about moving through it with clarity and ease, like a river carving its path. If you only rest and watch because you are too tired or afraid to try, you are merely sleeping while the world continues to turn around you.

Ask yourself: *Am I resting because I am full of energy and at peace, or am I resting because I am empty and afraid?* The answer will tell you whether you are truly following the Dao, or just pretending to.
