## Q6. Someone told me to steer, not paddle. I don't understand what that means for me.

Steering means guiding the boat toward the open sea, where you can feel the calm. Padding is keeping the boat close to the shore, close to the grip you hold. You're more eager to keep your own grip than to let the current take you toward the wider water. That's both a choice and a question.
